Shuai Liu, Ning Cao, Yile Chen, Yue Jiang, Gao Cong.

“NextLocMoE: Enhancing Next Location Prediction via Location-Semantics and Personalized Mixture-of-Experts” proposes an LLM-based framework that combines a Location Semantics MoE to represent the multiple functions of urban locations with a Personalized MoE to capture diverse user mobility patterns. A history-aware router uses long-term trajectories to guide expert selection, improving next-location prediction accuracy, cross-city generalization, and interpretability.
