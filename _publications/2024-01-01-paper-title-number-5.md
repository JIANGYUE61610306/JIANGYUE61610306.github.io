Shuai Liu, Xiucheng Li, Gao Cong, Yile Chen, Yue Jiang.

“Disentangling Dynamics: Advanced, Scalable and Explainable Imputation for Multivariate Time Series” introduces TIDER and AdaTIDER, which decompose multivariate time series into disentangled components (trend, seasonality, bias) and adaptively model cross-channel dependencies and multi-period seasonality to perform scalable, interpretable imputation.
